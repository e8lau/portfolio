I developed and published this as Speaker to resolve years of inconsistency in how constitutional and bylaw changes were implemented. The handbook details precise formatting hierarchies, amendment integration procedures, and archival best practices—ensuring that all ratified changes are incorporated consistently and preserved in perpetuity. This document remains the foundation for 7CSC’s amendment management system and is still referenced by Council officers today.
