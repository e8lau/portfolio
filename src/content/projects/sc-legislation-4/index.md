description: >-
  To improve organizational clarity and resolve systemic inconsistencies across the Council’s membership structure, I
  authored and passed this comprehensive amendment that reorganized and standardized all representative positions within
  7CSC’s governing documents. The legislation alphabetized all Title II positions, corrected inconsistent naming
  conventions between elected and appointed members, and added several missing representative roles, including the
  Student Health and Well-Being Representative and the Student Transportation Representative. This legislation
  significantly improved administrative transparency, simplified cross-committee collaboration, and established a clear
  reference structure that continues to define 7CSC’s membership today.
