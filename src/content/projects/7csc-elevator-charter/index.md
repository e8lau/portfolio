description: >-
  I co-authored the Charter establishing the Elevator Special Committee for Advocacy & Petition, an ad hoc body formed
  under the Seventh College Student Council to address persistent elevator failures that affected accessibility and
  safety for residents. Serving as a committee member, I focused on the digital and data infrastructure underpinning the
  initiative—designing QR reporting systems and conducting statistical analyses of maintenance response times. My
  findings demonstrated that the issue was not repair availability but delayed reporting between incidents and response,
  a gap that informed direct discussions with UC San Diego’s Housing, Dining, and Hospitality (HDH) department.
  Following our presentation, HDH acknowledged the procedural lapse and began improving their elevator issue reporting
  protocols. The initiative exemplified how targeted data analysis and digital tools can drive tangible institutional
  change within university systems.
