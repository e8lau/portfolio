  As the culminating piece of my legislative agenda as Speaker, Legislation 9 unified prior reforms into a comprehensive
  constitutional and bylaw update. It introduced the Vice President of Finance position to modernize fiscal oversight,
  codified interim appointment procedures to prevent administrative deadlock, and established a two-week Rules Committee
  review deadline to improve legislative efficiency. The legislation represented a holistic modernization of 7CSC
  governance—reinforcing accountability, procedural clarity, and organizational continuity. The reforms enacted under
  Legislation 9 continue to serve as the structural foundation of the Council’s operations today.
