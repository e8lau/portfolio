description: |
  As Program Chief Administrator for Woodside High School’s Freshman Transition Program, I authored and implemented this comprehensive restructuring initiative to address organizational inefficiencies and modernize digital communications. The legislation redesigned the entire leadership hierarchy—establishing defined roles for Chief Leaders, Section Leaders, and Head Period Leaders—and introduced a multi-tiered training model to improve accountability and operational clarity across seven class periods. I also led the program’s digital transition from Remind to Band, a more responsive communication platform, after analyzing engagement and notification data that highlighted severe inefficiencies in existing systems. Approved by the Program Chief Executive and ratified by the Process Group, this act served as the framework for future training operations and remains one of the most comprehensive structural reforms in the program’s history.
