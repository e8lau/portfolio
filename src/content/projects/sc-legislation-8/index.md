description: |
  Authored to correct structural inequities in representation, this amendment restructured the Council’s voting membership to ensure proportional representation for all student groups. By replacing the Synthesis Representative position with distinct First-Year and Transfer Representative roles, the legislation closed long-standing gaps that had left key student populations underrepresented. The reform passed unanimously and remains an essential component of the Council’s representational framework, reinforcing inclusivity and democratic participation within 7CSC governance.
