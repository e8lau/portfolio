  Submitted to the 7CSC Judicial Board, this inquiry identified multiple inconsistencies between the formatting of the Council’s Constitution and Bylaws and requested a formal ruling to authorize corrective action without additional legislation. I prepared the case to establish the Rules Committee’s procedural authority to align structural formatting across governing documents. The inquiry catalyzed later legislative reforms—most notably the adoption of the Amendment Handbook—and helped define the scope of technical versus interpretive authority within the Council’s judicial and rules framework.
