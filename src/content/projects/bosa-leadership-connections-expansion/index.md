  I authored this proposal to expand and formalize the responsibilities of the Leadership Connections Committee within the Woodside High School Leadership Program. The document redefined the committee’s role from a limited communications group into a central coordination body managing resources and inter-committee collaboration. My framework introduced monthly cross-section reporting, structured task routing, and communication protocols to reduce administrative bottlenecks during distance learning. Approved by the Board of Student Affairs and Student Activities Directors, the policy institutionalized a sustainable reporting pipeline that strengthened transparency and operational efficiency across all program sections.
