description: >-
  As Speaker, I identified the absence of a formal mechanism for amending the Council’s Constitution as a major
  procedural vulnerability. To address this, I authored and successfully passed Legislation 5B, which introduced a
  dedicated amendments clause outlining the conditions and thresholds required for future constitutional changes. The
  reform established a three-fourths supermajority requirement, delegated limited formatting authority to the Rules
  Committee, and required all amendments to undergo formal introduction and review before ratification. This legislation
  remains one of the most structurally important pieces passed during my tenure, creating the legal and procedural
  foundation for all future constitutional revisions.
