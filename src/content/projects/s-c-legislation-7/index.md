  In my ongoing effort to strengthen administrative continuity, I sponsored and passed this amendment to synchronize the
  appointment cycle for key representative positions with the Spring Quarter schedule. This shift allowed for smoother
  transitions between outgoing and incoming representatives and ensured that appointments occurred with sufficient time
  for training and onboarding before the academic year’s end. The reform continues to provide structural stability,
  enabling consistent representation and operational readiness across annual leadership changes.
