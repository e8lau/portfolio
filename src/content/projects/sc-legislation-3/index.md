description: |
  This legislation, which I authored and carried through the full approval process, was designed to strengthen transparency and accountability in the Council’s legislative process. It expanded the Rules Committee’s jurisdiction from mere review to formal amendment authority, allowing it to ensure that all proposed legislation met constitutional and formatting standards before Council ratification. I introduced clear procedural requirements mandating that all constitutional and bylaw amendments be reviewed by the Rules Committee prior to voting, thereby closing procedural gaps that had caused confusion in prior sessions. Today, this framework continues to serve as the procedural backbone for how legislation is submitted, reviewed, and enacted in 7CSC.
