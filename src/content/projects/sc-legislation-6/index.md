description: |
  This amendment targeted a specific procedural limitation within Council meetings: the time allotted for Open Forum discussions. Recognizing that the existing structure constrained student participation, I proposed and passed Legislation 6 to extend the maximum duration of Open Forum from ten to twenty-five minutes. This change enabled greater constituent engagement, allowed for deeper discussion on complex issues, and established a more inclusive format for community feedback—while maintaining orderly proceedings through the existing two-thirds vote extension rule. The reform was adopted unanimously and continues to facilitate open dialogue at 7CSC sessions.
