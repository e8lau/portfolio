description: >-
  As Speaker, I authored this judicial inquiry to resolve a foundational constitutional ambiguity: whether the Seventh
  College Student Council possessed the authority to amend its own Constitution. The document analyzed competing
  procedural interpretations and cited constitutional and bylaw provisions to construct a defensible amendment pathway
  through the Rules Committee. This case established the procedural basis that later informed the creation of a formal
  amendments clause (Legislation 5B) and directly influenced the Council’s modern constitutional process. It remains a
  key precedent in 7CSC’s internal legal history.
