  As a Classical Pianist under instructor Marta Schermerhorn, I earned every available award in the Music Teachers’ Association of California’s Certificate of Merit (CM) program each year I was eligible—spanning twelve consecutive years of study. This consistent record of full-award achievement reflects both exceptional musical precision and long-term discipline that have continued to influence my creative, analytical, and professional pursuits.
