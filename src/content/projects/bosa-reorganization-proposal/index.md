description: |
  As a member of the Woodside High School Board of Student Affairs, I authored this proposal to reorganize and modernize the school’s Student Activities webpage. The project analyzed the existing web architecture, identified information bottlenecks between student government and activity pages, and introduced a dual-page system separating “Student Activities” from “Student Government.” I also designed a framework for embedded Google Docs integration that enabled remote updates without administrator intervention—a solution conceived during the early COVID-19 lockdowns. The proposal was unanimously approved by the Student Activities Directors and the Board of Student Affairs, establishing the structural basis for all subsequent online governance materials at Woodside.
