description: >-
  As Speaker of the Seventh College Student Council, I authored and sponsored this foundational piece of legislation to
  address long-standing inconsistencies in how committees operated and were chaired across the organization. The
  amendment redefined chairmanship authority, created a formal process for temporarily ceding leadership, and
  standardized quorum definitions to ensure representative participation across voting and non-voting members. I also
  consolidated and clarified committee membership requirements to strengthen operational efficiency. The legislation
  passed with broad support and remains a cornerstone of 7CSC’s structural framework, establishing the foundation for
  uniform governance practices that subsequent councils continue to rely on.
