    About Hi, I'm Ethan

    Building smarter strategies with data and insight

    I’ve always been drawn to patterns—whether in numbers, stories, or systems. What started as curiosity became a passion for using data to understand people, solve problems, and build things that matter. In exploring new industries and passions, I am still learning—but I thrive when turning complexity into clarity and insight into action.

    How I Got Here

    My journey began at Woodside High School, where I served as ASB President and developed an early appreciation for how institutions operate—and how they can be improved through thoughtful leadership. That curiosity deepened at the University of California—San Diego, where I pursued a degree in Management Science, an economics program focused on game theory and optimization. I complemented it with a minor in Data Science to strengthen my quantitative and technical foundation.

    I have continued to nurture those interests through hands-on experiences that challenge me to grow. I co-founded a startup, led legislative reform on campus, and took on roles in both consulting and venture capital. I analyzed million-record datasets, contributed to strategic planning for real clients, and collaborated with entrepreneurs shaping early-stage ventures. Each chapter built on the last, deepening my interest in solving real-world problems through structure, insight, and thoughtful execution.

    My Values & Beliefs

    Analytical Rigor

    Good strategy starts with structure. Precision brings clarity to ambiguity, helping surface the right questions before rushing to answers. In environments shaped by complexity or uncertainty, rigorous analysis doesn’t just support decision-making—it earns trust and drives action.

    Strategic Empathy

    Empathy turns insight into impact. The best ideas often come from understanding what isn’t being said—the tensions, motivations, and needs beneath the surface. Direction informed by empathy isn’t diluted; it’s sharpened by context and guided by purpose.

    Initiative with Intention

    Meaningful progress comes from paying attention—spotting what could be better and stepping up to improve it. When initiative is grounded in reflection and purpose, it becomes more than action—it becomes momentum that others can build on.

    Curiosity as a Discipline

    Curiosity becomes powerful when it’s consistent. The best learning happens through iteration—trying, refining, questioning again. Treated as a habit, not a phase, curiosity shapes sharper thinkers and more adaptive teams.

    What I Bring to the Table

    I bring a developing blend of analytical thinking, strategic awareness, and collaborative leadership. I’ve had the opportunity to explore roles that challenged me to think critically and contribute meaningfully. From designing machine learning models to improve alumni outreach, to supporting startup strategy and revising campus governance structures, I’ve worked to translate complexity into clarity and turn questions into action.

    I tend to approach problems with both structure and openness—balancing the drive to solve quickly with the patience to listen, iterate, and refine. I’ve learned that good ideas often emerge from unexpected places, and I try to create space for those ideas to surface.

    While I’m proud of the experiences I’ve had, I’m just as focused on what I have yet to learn. I aim to bring a mindset of thoughtful contribution to every project I join—adding value not just through my skills, but through my willingness to listen, improve, and help the team move forward.

    Beyond the Résumé

    There’s more to me than data models and strategy decks. I’ve always had a broad curiosity—drawn to patterns in cities, conversations, and code. I enjoy photography, dancing, tinkering with web tools, and finding small ways to improve everyday systems. I find that creativity and structure often feed into each other in unexpected ways.

    Much of what I’ve done hasn’t come from being the most experienced person in the room—it’s come from asking questions, identifying gaps, and offering to help. I’ve stepped into roles not because I had to, but because I saw something worth improving or contributing to.

    I don’t pretend to have all the answers, and I don’t rush to fill every silence. But I do bring a consistent curiosity, a commitment to integrity, and a deep respect for the teams and systems I’m part of. I’m excited about where I’m headed, and grateful for the process of learning and contributing along the way.
